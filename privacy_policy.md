# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 31th, 2026

## Overview

Buzzosaurus ("the App") is developed by Corentin Dechaud ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

## No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage data from users of this App. Specifically:

- We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information.
- We do not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or analytics data.
- We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- We do not store any data on external servers.
- Any data created or used within the App (e.g. local ips, player names) stays on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

## Third-Party Services

This App does not integrate any third-party services that collect data.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

If our data practices ever change, we will update this Privacy Policy and revise the "Last updated" date above.
